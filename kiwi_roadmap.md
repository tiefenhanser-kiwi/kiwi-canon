## Recipe catalog & wizard-freshness plan (Hans-ruled July 6, 2026)

**Origin:** AI cost at scale (~$0.40–0.70/plan, dominated by the Sonnet expansion stack — ~$2,000–3,500/mo at the 1K-user scenario; cross-ref D-WS7-077) plus UX complaints: repetitive plan names ("Mexican Fiesta Week"), same meals week after week, single-cuisine plans. **Phase A — Wizard prompt tuning (prompt-body-only; July 9: rides alongside the Phase B close inside the WS7-8 wrap, or parks as a WS9-downtime block; pairs with D-WS7-166/-167):**
